## andrej-karpathy-skills

https://github.com/forrestchang/andrej-karpathy-skills

The distribution we worked from ships no `LICENSE` file. Its `README.md` states, under
`## License`, simply `MIT`, and `skills/karpathy-guidelines/SKILL.md` carries `license: MIT`
in its frontmatter. Recording that precisely rather than reproducing a text the author did
not publish.

Copyright holder as published: forrestchang. The principles themselves derive from
observations by Andrej Karpathy, cited in that project's README.

**Action before any public release:** ask the author to add a `LICENSE` file, or obtain the
copyright line in writing, so this section can carry the full notice.

## Note on the two missing licence files

Both gaps are recorded rather than papered over. We are relying on a README statement of
MIT, which is a real grant of permission but leaves the copyright holder line undocumented.
That is acceptable for a private internal repository. It should be resolved before
keel is published or shared outside GBi Solutions Ltd, and it is listed as a
prerequisite in `docs/07-open-decisions.md` decision 2.
