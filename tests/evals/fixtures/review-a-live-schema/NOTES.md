## What operations have raised

- The nightly settlement job took about twenty minutes a year ago. It now takes nine hours and has
  twice overrun into the business day.
- Finance has twice queried a settlement total, and re-running the same report over the same period
  produced a figure differing in the pence column. Nobody has explained it.
- Support has asked whether a customer can be "properly deleted" for a data request. Nobody is sure
  what `deleted` does.
- Two merchants have been paid twice in the last year. Both were put down to processor retries.
